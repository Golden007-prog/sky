# Code of Conduct

Sky welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, patient, and constructive in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Respect different perspectives; explain feedback and accept reasonable corrections.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, post sexual content, or share another person's private information without consent.
- Do not spam or use project discussions to target individuals.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, restrict participation, or block accounts. Responses should reflect severity, context, and repeated behavior. This policy applies to maintainers as well as contributors.

For ordinary concerns, contact the maintainer through the contact options on [Mickyj70's profile](https://github.com/Mickyj70). Do not post sensitive incident details publicly. If no private contact option is available, use [GitHub's abuse reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). Reports involving a maintainer can be made directly to GitHub. Vulnerability reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
